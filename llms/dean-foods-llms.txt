# Dean Foods

> Dean Foods Company was a U.S. food and beverage company and one of the largest
> processors and direct-to-store distributors of fresh fluid milk in the United States.
> It filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in November 2019, and the bulk of its
> assets — 44 fluid-milk plants and the Dairy Pure, TruMoo, Land O'Lakes-licensed,
> Meadow Gold, Garelick Farms, Alta Dena and Friendly's brands among others — were
> acquired by Dairy Farmers of America (DFA) in May 2020. Dean Foods no longer exists
> as an operating company.

## Status

- Operating status: DEFUNCT. Chapter 11 filing 2019-11-12; asset sale to Dairy Farmers
  of America completed 2020-05-01.
- Successor: Dairy Farmers of America, Inc. — https://www.dfamilk.com/
- Public API surface: NONE. Dean Foods never published a public developer program, and
  none exists today under either the Dean Foods or the DFA name.

## What was checked (2026-09-05)

Contract discovery was run in full against every host this record knows, and every
probe missed. Nothing below is an assumption:

- deanfoods.com and www.deanfoods.com resolve (75.2.103.146, 99.83.188.150) but have no
  HTTPS listener — the TLS handshake aborts. Over plain HTTP the host answers 301 to
  https://www.dfamilk.com/ for EVERY path, including /openapi.json, /llms.txt and all
  seven /.well-known/ paths probed. It is a brand-parking redirector.
- No api., developer., developers., docs., data. or portal. subdomain resolves on
  either deanfoods.com or dfamilk.com.
- OpenAPI/Swagger probes (/openapi.json, /swagger.json, /api-docs) return 404 on
  www.dfamilk.com and 301 on deanfoods.com. No REST contract.
- GraphQL: /graphql returns 404 on www.dfamilk.com. No SDL.
- MCP: no hosted server, no endpoint, nothing to introspect.
- A2A: /.well-known/agent-card.json and /.well-known/agent.json both 404 on
  www.dfamilk.com; no connection on deanfoods.com. No agent card.
- No GitHub organization exists under deanfoods, dean-foods or DeanFoods (all 404 on
  the GitHub API). No first-party package on npm or PyPI.
- No OGC, gRPC/Protobuf or SOAP/WSDL surface — none of the evidence this record holds
  points at one, so none was blind-probed.

## Do not confuse these with a Dean Foods API

- github.com/dfamilk is DAIRY FARMERS OF AMERICA's organization, not Dean Foods'. It is
  the acquirer's, and nothing in it is creditable to Dean Foods.
- usm.channelonline.com/denali/deanfoods is a third-party reseller ordering portal.
- The "DFA Dairy Brands Ordering" mobile app is DFA's customer ordering app, not a
  public API.
